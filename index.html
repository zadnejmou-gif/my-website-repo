08FAQ

Quick answers.

Who delivers my order?

A UBMA student whose student ID has been verified by DropBy. You see their name and rating before they arrive.

How do I pay?

In cash, to the courier, when you receive your order. For pickup orders you pay at the restaurant counter.

How much is delivery?

Between 100 and 150 DA depending on the distance. The exact amount is shown before you confirm.

What if no courier accepts?

The order is cancelled automatically after a few minutes and you pay nothing.

Can I pick up my order myself?

Yes. Pre-order, choose a time, and collect it at the counter with no delivery fee.

Is DropBy run by the university?

No. DropBy is an independent student-led initiative at Université Badji Mokhtar Annaba.
